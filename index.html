      What's on the board

      Clock and focus timer

      Start counts up from zero for as long as you work; Done logs the minutes to the day, the block, and the deadline it was for. Prefer rounds? Switch to 25-minute rounds in full screen.

      Full screen: tap the timer to fill the screen with it, along with the block and its next step. Tap the big time or Exit to come back. It stays full screen if the page reloads.

      The squares under the timer count today's finished rounds. During a learning block the break first says what to digest (for example "add it to your map").

      Now, Next and Tomorrow

      Now is the block you're in and Next is the next one today. With 30+ free minutes before it, Free until … shows the most urgent thing to start instead, and Start puts the timer on it. Late at night Tomorrow shows your first block of the next day.

      • A step. Tap it when it's done; it's ticked on the calendar too.
      • A digest step: process what you just read before reading more.
      • "2/4 steps done". Tap it to untick the last step.
      • How much of the block's time has passed.

      The letters in the corner say what kind of learning the block is, and how to digest it:

      • PProcedural: practice it right away
      • AAnalogous: critique the comparison
      • CConceptual: add it to your map
      • EEvidence: store it, rehearse later
      • RReference: make a flashcard

      Due

      Everything due in the next six weeks: all of Canvas, plus calendar events with "due", "deadline" or "submit" in the title, exams and quizzes (not "review" or "prep" sessions), and all-day events like "Pset 6" or "Essay 3". When two entries are the same assignment, the earlier date and time win.

      Anything due in the next 36 hours and the next exam or quiz always get a row, even if that means more rows. The last line says how many more there are and what's next. A red line at the top means a calendar didn't load or the board is offline, so something could be missing.

      The right column is time planned: hours already blocked on your Study Calendar before it's due. Only the most urgent thing with nothing blocked gets a red 0h. The dark row is due today. Once a due time passes the row fades and says "passed" until the day ends.

      Finished early? Tap the row, then tap it again: it's marked done here, on the desktop board and on the widget.

      Check-ins

      Once a day, after 21:00 and your last block, the dark strip asks about the day: All done logs every block as done. Not all goes through them one at a time (Done, Partly or Missed), then offers to move what's left to free slots. If you never answer, the blocks count as done.

      Sunday evening and Monday it offers to move last week's unfinished blocks into the new week, or let them go. After four days without a tap anywhere it asks if you want a fresh start.

      When nothing needs you (no block on or coming up, nothing due today) the board dims and only the clock and the next block stay bright.

      How long things take

      From your night check-ins over the last three weeks (blocks you didn't mark count as done). "×1.6" means blocks like these need about 1.6 times the time you give them. The same numbers are written into your next Weekly review, so planning can use them.

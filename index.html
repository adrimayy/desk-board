      What's on the board

      Clock and focus timer

      Start runs a 25-minute focus round. When it ends the board chimes and a 5-minute break starts on its own; the next round waits for Start. Reset goes back to a fresh 25:00.

      The squares under the timer count today's finished rounds. During a learning block the break first says what to digest (for example "add it to your map").

      Now, Next and Tomorrow

      Now is the block you're in, Next is the next one today, and late at night Tomorrow shows your first block of the next day.

      • A step. Tap it when it's done; it's ticked on the calendar too.
      • A digest step: process what you just read before reading more.
      • "2/4 steps done". Tap it to untick the last step.
      • How much of the block's time has passed.

      The letters in the corner say what kind of learning the block is, and how to digest it:

      • PProcedural: practice it right away
      • AAnalogous: critique the comparison
      • CConceptual: add it to your map
      • EEvidence: store it, rehearse later
      • RReference: make a flashcard

      Due

      Everything due in the next two weeks, from Canvas and from calendar events with "due", "quiz", "exam", "test" or "midterm" in the title. When Canvas and your calendar disagree, Canvas wins.

      The right column is time planned: hours already blocked on your Study Calendar before it's due. A red 0h means nothing is blocked yet. The dark row is due today; a faded row's time has passed.

      Check-ins

      After a Study block ends, the dark strip asks how it went. Done, Partly or Missed is written on the block and colors it green, yellow or red. After Partly or Missed it offers to move what's left to the next free slot (never into sleep, 12–8 AM). "Follow-through" is today's done blocks and this week's average.

      How long things take

      From your check-ins over the last three weeks. "×1.6" means blocks like these need about 1.6 times the time you give them. The same numbers are written into your next Weekly review, so planning can use them.
